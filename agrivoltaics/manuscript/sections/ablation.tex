To isolate the contribution of the stress-aware inputs, we trained a variant whose control network sees only phenology, time, design and crop, with soil water, ARID, the maximum-temperature, irradiance and ET$_0$ forecasts masked. Both variants were trained for the same budget of 200 steps and evaluated on the held-out seasons. At similar mean retention (0.898 vs 0.897 at present climate), removing the stress inputs reduced the relative electricity from 0.69 to 0.50 and the LER from 1.59 to 1.40. Under +3\,\textdegree C the drop was from 1.67 to 1.42. The stress-blind policy performs like the expert rules: it can learn \emph{when} in the season to share light, but not to respond to the heat and drought of a particular season. Training longer (400 vs 200 steps) added a further 0.04--0.08 to the LER.
