Highlights
- Daily light sharing lets trackers carry 2-3x the module area at the same 90% yield retention.
- A phenology rule is the best simple controller and needs no weather forecast.
- Shade response learned from 428 field observations; between-study spread about 0.22 in relative yield.
- Learned control adds 0.018 LER over a tuned phenology rule; ensemble training and biomass feedback do not help.
- A floor raised by 0.02 performs like an ensemble-quantile design at equal compliance; neither is safe outside the ensemble.
- Under illustrative costs, light sharing breaks even at 70-92 USD/MWh (static arrays 59); a conventional PV plant is more profitable above 48.
