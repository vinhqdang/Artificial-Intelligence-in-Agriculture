Highlights
- SALS learns agrivoltaic array design and daily tracker control end-to-end.
- A differentiable simulator couples PV, crop light, microclimate and crop growth.
- On held-out sites, the land-equivalent ratio rose from 1.42 (expert rule) to 1.63.
- The learned policy shades crops in heat; its advantage grows under +3 °C warming.
- 5% of staple cropland could supply about 31,000 TWh of electricity per year.
