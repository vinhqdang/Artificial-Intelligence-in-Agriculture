\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.6em]
\item Light sharing lets trackers carry 2--3$\times$ more modules at a strict 90\% yield floor.
\item A neural controller adds 0.007 LER over a tuned window, null at equal retention.
\item Mean-response designs leave 70\% of pairs at the floor; an oracle reaches 87\%.
\item Conformal margins hold inside the ensemble; a learned design does worse.
\item Even with the mean met, 24--31\% of seasons miss the 90\% yield floor.
\end{itemize}
