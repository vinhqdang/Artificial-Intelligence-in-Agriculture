\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.6em]
\item Light sharing allows 2--3$\times$ denser trackers at a 90\% floor (proportional response).
\item A learned shade response is no better than proportional loss for staple crops.
\item A neural controller adds 0.007 LER over a tuned window (0.003 at equal retention).
\item Design margins matter: 70\% vs 85--87\% of pairs at the floor (mean vs oracle).
\item Plants beat agrivoltaics above 48 USD/MWh (64 at equal cost per watt).
\end{itemize}
