\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.6em]
\item Differentiable simulator trains agrivoltaic tracker control by back-propagation.
\item A neural controller adds 0.007 LER over a tuned window, null at equal retention.
\item Conformal margins keep compliance in-ensemble; a learned design network is worse.
\item Shade-response uncertainty, not the learner, limits yield-floor compliance.
\item Rule-based light sharing allows 2--3$\times$ denser trackers at a 90\% yield floor.
\end{itemize}
